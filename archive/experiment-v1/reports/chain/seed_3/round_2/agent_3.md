# Critical Assessment: Misapplied Integrability and Overlooked Bijective Structure

## The False Free-Fermionic Premise

The report's central claim that KTW puzzles "are NOT free-fermionic" fundamentally **misunderstands the bijection problem**. Free-fermionic structure is neither necessary nor claimed for growth diagram constructions. The author conflates:

1. **Vertex model R-matrices** (spectral parameter dependent)
2. **Growth diagram transition rules** (combinatorial, parameter-free)
3. **Bijective correspondences** (equality of cardinalities)

Classical growth diagrams for RSK, Burge correspondence, and other bijections work precisely because they **avoid** spectral parameters entirely. The "12→3 compression" isn't about fermion modes—it's about **local jeu de taquin equivalence classes** where multiple puzzle configurations yield identical tableau contributions.

## Yang-Baxter: Irrelevant Sophistication

The Yang-Baxter equation requirement is a **category error**. We're not seeking integrable vertex models but **combinatorial bijections**. The relevant consistency condition is **confluence of local moves**, already established in Purbhoo's migration algorithm through:

- **Hexagonal flip commutativity** (geometric)
- **Jeu de taquin commutation** (tableau-theoretic)  
- **Mosaic transformation uniqueness** (bijective)

Yang-Baxter equations govern **quantum integrable systems**. KTW puzzles are **classical combinatorial objects**. The confluence required for bijections is **weaker** than Yang-Baxter consistency.

## The Missing Migration Connection

The report completely ignores **Purbhoo's established bijection machinery**. The question isn't whether some hypothetical vertex model works—it's whether growth diagrams can **replicate existing migration moves** more directly.

Specifically:
- Migration transforms **colored rhombi** through **local hexagonal flips**
- Each flip corresponds to a **jeu de taquin slide** in the target tableau
- The **reading order** of rhombi determines **tableau entry placement**

A growth diagram approach would need face labels tracking **rhombus flow** through the migration path, not "spectral parameters" or "qKZ residues."

## Spectral Mystification vs. Combinatorial Reality

The author's focus on "spectral data," "eigenvalues," and "qKZ connections" is **sophisticated-sounding but irrelevant**. The LR coefficients c^λ_{μν} appear as:

- **Integer counts** of puzzle configurations
- **Cardinalities** of LR tableau sets  
- **Degrees** of Grassmannian intersection multiplicities

No spectral parameters, quantum groups, or monodromy required. The "quantum-to-classical limit" interpretation mistakes the **representation-theoretic significance** of LR numbers for their **combinatorial definition**.

## Boundary Conditions: The Geometric Reality

KTW puzzles have **fixed rhombus edge types** on the triangular boundary. This isn't about "domain wall integrability" but about **boundary value problems** in combinatorial bijections. The constraint is **geometric** (edge matching) not **algebraic** (spectral).

The "three-fold symmetry" mentioned doesn't "break integrability"—it's the **defining symmetry** of the KTW puzzle construction, inherited from the **hexagonal rhombus tilings** of the mosaic interpretation.

## The Core Challenge: Locality vs. Global Structure

The fundamental issue the report misses: growth diagrams require **local face computation rules**, but migration involves **global rhombus movement**. The question is whether:

1. **Local puzzle piece information** sufficiently determines tableau growth
2. **Face label updates** can track rhombus positions without full migration
3. **Jeu de taquin slide effects** can be computed from adjacent face data

This is about **information propagation** in discrete dynamical systems, not quantum integrability.

- Puzzle pieces as **flow sources/sinks**  
- Migration paths as **flow conservation**

- Growth diagram faces as **flow state variables**

The bijection question becomes: can **local flow conservation laws** replicate **global migration dynamics** through **incremental face updates**?
