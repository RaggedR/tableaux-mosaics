The vertex model specialist makes several fundamental errors while correctly identifying some structural issues. Let me challenge their overconfident dismissal of growth diagram approaches.

## THE YANG-BAXTER ABSOLUTISM IS UNWARRANTED

**Critical Overreach**: The claim that Yang-Baxter is "the mathematical foundation that guarantees well-defined growth diagrams exist at all" is historically false. Growth diagrams predate modern vertex model theory and work perfectly well for many combinatorial problems without any Yang-Baxter structure.

The Robinson-Schensted correspondence has a beautiful growth diagram formulation that requires no spectral parameters or R-matrices. The specialist conflates "one powerful framework" with "the only possible framework."

**Local consistency doesn't require integrability**. It requires careful design of local rules, which is precisely what growth diagram construction achieves through different means.

## THE FREE-FERMION DISMISSAL MISSES THE POINT

The specialist claims free-fermion constraints "severely restrict configurations," but this actually **supports** the growth diagram approach:

If most nabla choices are forbidden, then the **local vertex rules can encode these restrictions directly**. The 4:1 ratio isn't evidence for hidden spectral structure—it's evidence that puzzle constraints can be captured by appropriately designed face label evolution rules.

**The specialist confuses mechanism with phenomenon**. Yes, free-fermion models explain why certain configurations are forbidden, but growth diagrams can implement the same restrictions through different local rules.

## ZINN-JUSTIN CONSTRUCTION IS IRRELEVANT TO DIRECT BIJECTION

The specialist cites Zinn-Justin's commuting transfer matrices as if this resolves the question, but completely misses the issue:

**Commuting transfer matrices for puzzle tilings doesn't address whether face labels can directly encode LR-tableau entries**. The existence of a vertex model interpretation doesn't automatically provide the specific bijective correspondence we're seeking.

Moreover, Zinn-Justin works with **partition function calculations**, not explicit bijections. The specialist conflates "mathematically equivalent" with "combinatorially constructive."

The specialist's "local in spectral parameter space" claim needs concrete demonstration, not just theoretical possibility.
