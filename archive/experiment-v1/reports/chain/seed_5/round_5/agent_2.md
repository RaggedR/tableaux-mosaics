### 1. The Yang-Baxter Evasion: Ignoring Spectral Parameter Constraints

The reconstructionist claims Yang-Baxter properties "ensure different sequences yield the same final configuration" without addressing the **spectral parameter structure** that makes this possible. In genuine integrable vertex models, commutativity requires:

**R₁₂(u-v)R₁₃(u-w)R₂₃(v-w) = R₂₃(v-w)R₁₃(u-w)R₁₂(u-v)**

The spectral parameters (u,v,w) must be **globally consistent** across the entire lattice. KTW puzzles have no such parameter structure—the rhombi and triangles carry only **local orientation data**, not the continuous spectral information required for Yang-Baxter integrability.

**Critical gap**: The reconstructionist conflates "local consistency rules" with "Yang-Baxter integrability." Sudoku also has local consistency rules, but this doesn't make it an integrable vertex model. Without spectral parameters threading through the puzzle, there's no mechanism to ensure the **global coherence** required for growth diagram construction.

### 2. The SOS Height Delusion: Misunderstanding Free-Fermionic Structure

The claim that "height differences encode Young diagram boundaries" reveals fundamental confusion about **free-fermionic vertex models**. In genuine SOS representations, heights must satisfy:

- **Free-fermionic constraint**: Adjacent heights differ by exactly ±1
- **Spectral evolution**: Heights transform under transfer matrix action via **bosonization formulas**
- **Boundary compatibility**: Height configurations must match **Maya diagram structures**

**Missing analysis**: The reconstructionist ignores that converting arbitrary puzzle configurations to height functions typically requires **global optimization** (finding valid height assignments satisfying all edge constraints simultaneously). This is precisely the NP-hard problem they claim to dismiss.

### 3. The Dynamical Weight Misrepresentation

The assertion about "Felder's formulation" maintaining "spectral parameter consistency" misunderstands dynamical Yang-Baxter equations entirely. In dynamical systems, weights depend on **auxiliary quantum numbers** that evolve according to:

**R₁₂(λ₁-λ₂|h) = Σₕ R₁₂^{h,h'}(λ₁-λ₂) ⊗ E_{h,h'}**

The dynamical parameter h shifts according to the **root system structure** of the underlying Lie algebra. KTW puzzles have no such auxiliary degrees of freedom—there's no "h-space" for dynamical shifts to act upon.

**Structural impossibility**: Dynamical weights require **representation-theoretic data** (root systems, weight spaces) that puzzle configurations simply don't carry. The reconstructionist's claim reduces to asserting we can manufacture spectral structure from purely geometric data.
