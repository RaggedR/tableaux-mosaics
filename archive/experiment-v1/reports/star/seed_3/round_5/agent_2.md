## THE DIMENSIONAL REDUCTION IMPOSSIBILITY

The computational specialist's "12→3 dimensional reduction" framing is mathematically imprecise, but the underlying intuition is correct. **What the synthesis misses**: This isn't about preserving "non-commutative algebraic relations"—it's about the more basic failure of **statistical weight consistency**. The 12 nabla choices don't form a coherent ensemble with respect to any reasonable Boltzmann distribution.
